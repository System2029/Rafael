HIGHLIGHTS: Nora Aunor, the Philippine Superstar’s state funeral

Nora Aunor, the legendary Filipino actress and singer who inspired generations of fans, died on Wednesday, April 16. She was 71.

A National Artist, Aunor will be honored with a fitting state funeral on Tuesday, April 22, at the Libingan ng mga Bayani in Taguig City, after a decorated career that includes iconic films such as Himala (1992).
